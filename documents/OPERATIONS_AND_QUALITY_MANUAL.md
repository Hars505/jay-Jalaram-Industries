# Operations & Quality Assurance Manual: Jay Jalaram Industries

This operational manual outlines the standard operating procedures (SOP), build-to-print workflow, quality assurance protocols, and environmental health & safety (EHS) guidelines implemented at Jay Jalaram Industries.

---

## 1. Build-To-Print Operational Workflow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    CustomerDrawing["1. Drawing / CAD Received"] --> ProcessPlanning["2. Process & Tooling Plan"]
    ProcessPlanning --> CNCSetup["3. CNC / Lathe Roughing"]
    CNCSetup --> HeatTreatment["4. Heat Treatment (if required)"]
    HeatTreatment --> FinishGrinding["5. Finish Grinding (Rotary/Centreless)"]
    FinishGrinding --> QualityInsp["6. Quality Inspection & Gauging"]
    QualityInsp --> Packaging["7. Cleaning, Oiling & Dispatch"]
```

### Operational Steps
1. **Inquiry & Drawing Analysis**: Customer engineering drawing or sample part is reviewed for material grade, heat treatment requirements, geometric tolerances (GD&T), and batch size.
2. **Process Planning & Fixturing**: Selection of raw material (bar stock, forging, or casting), tooling inserts, CNC program generation, and custom fixture setup.
3. **Primary Machining**: Rough turning on 4.5" centre lathes or rough pocketing/milling on Leadwell V40 / Haas VF-2 CNC machines under flood coolant.
4. **Heat Treatment / Surface Coating**: Hardening, stress-relieving, induction hardening, or black oxide coating as per specification.
5. **Precision Grinding**: Finishing diameters on centreless/cylindrical grinders or flat faces on rotary surface grinders down to micron tolerances.
6. **Final Quality Metrology**: 100% inspection of critical dimensions on the granite surface plate using dial indicators, bore gauges, and micrometers.
7. **Washing, Anti-Rust & Packing**: Cleaning in component cleaning units, applying rust-preventative oil, and protective boxing for transport.

## 2. Quality Control & Tolerance Standards

### Precision Standards
- **Milling Envelope**: Up to 1000 × 500 × 500 mm (Leadwell V40).
- **Rotary Grinding Envelope**: Up to Ø1000 × 200 mm (Pinnacle Rotary Surface Grinder).
- **Bar Diameter Turning**: Precision turning down to 10 mm bar diameter.
- **Grinding Tolerances**: Concentricity, parallelism, and surface finish held within tight engineering tolerances.

### Calibration & Record Keeping
- Inspection instruments are calibrated against master standards at regular intervals.
- Job cards track heat treatment batches, operator signatures, and final inspection clearance reports.

---

## 3. Workplace Safety & Environmental Handling

1. **Machine Guarding & Operator Safety**: All CNC machines operate with interlocked safety doors and flood coolant splash guards. Machinists wear safety glasses and protective footwear.
2. **Coolant & Waste Management**: Used machining coolants and cutting oils are collected in sealed disposal tanks for safe environmental handling.
3. **Metal Chip Recycling**: Steel, bronze, and aluminum turnings/chips are segregated in dedicated scrap bins for recycling.
