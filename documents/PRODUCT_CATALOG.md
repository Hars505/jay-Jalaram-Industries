## Detailed Product Descriptions

### 1. Shafts & Threaded Motion Drive Components
- **Worm Shaft**: Custom ground worm shafts manufactured with integrated or pressed bronze collars, engineered for low backlash and high wear resistance in gear reduction gearboxes.
- **Multi-Start Lead Screw**: Hardened and ground lead screws featuring multi-start stacked thread profiles for fast linear transmission with smooth engagement.
- **Long Precision Shaft**: Slender turned and centreless ground shafts up to 1000mm length, eliminating runout and maintaining exact cylindrical concentricity.
- **Stepped Pin**: Complex multi-diameter locating pins with hexagonal head or hex drive ends, heat-treated for high shearing resistance.

### 2. Gears & Splines
- **Spur Gear**: High-precision spur gears with broached internal keyways, generated or CNC milled tooth flanks for smooth power transmission.
- **Fine-Pitch Gear**: High tooth count disc gears with black oxide chemical coating for corrosion protection and anti-glare finish.
- **Splined Hub**: Heavy-duty drive hubs featuring broached or shaped internal splines with hardened bores to withstand high torque loads.

### 3. Bushes, Rollers & Mounting Hardware
- **Flanged Bush**: Custom bronze or steel flanged bushes with square or circular mounting flanges, bored and precision reamed for sleeve bearing applications.
- **Spline Roller Assembly**: Assembly unit comprising a knurled roller body pressed onto high-precision ground shaft ends for conveyor and feed roller machinery.
- **Mounting Block**: Solid steel or aluminum mounting blocks milled on 3-axis CNC machining centres, featuring tight tolerance bored holes and counter-bored mounting slots.
